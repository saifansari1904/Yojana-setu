## EXCLUSION METHODOLOGY & CORE PRINCIPLES

Yojana Setu is built strictly as an **Entrepreneur Business + Government Scheme + Funding Intelligence Platform**. 
In accordance with product mandates, the following categories were systematically excluded:

1. **Student Scholarships & Educational Grants**: Pre/post-matric scholarships, overseas studies grants, hostel aid, fee reimbursement (excluded: direct student welfare without commercial enterprise formation).
2. **Social Security & Destitute Pensions**: Old-age pensions, widow stipends, disability welfare allowances, folk artist pensions (excluded: non-commercial social transfers).
3. **Residential Housing & Plots**: Free house sites, slum rehabilitation, housing board construction grants (excluded: personal shelter rather than commercial premises).
4. **Healthcare, Hospitalization & Dialysis**: Critical illness packages, CM health insurance, free dialysis schemes, dispensaries (excluded: individual health protection).
5. **Food Rations & Commodity Gifts**: Subsidized canteens, grain distribution, festival gift bags, midday meal programs (excluded: non-commercial consumption safety nets).
6. **Marriage Assistance & Girl Child Doles**: Shaadi Mubarak, Kalyana Lakshmi, marriage doles (excluded: personal marital gifts).
7. **Unemployment Allowances & Job-Seeker Training**: Cash stipends for unemployed youth or corporate job placement training (excluded: job-seeker aid rather than entrepreneur capital).
8. **Disaster Relief & Crop Loss Compensation**: Drought relief, flood rehabilitation, loan forgiveness (excluded: ex-post relief rather than enterprise credit).
9. **National Scheme Duplication**: Schemes like PM SVANidhi or PM Vishwakarma duplicated under state labels (excluded: already maintained in Yojana Setu Central Scheme Intelligence).
